Architecture & FAIR Interoperability

IBNP-DB integrates an automated Tier A chemical-genomic background universe (13,459 bacterial small molecules from NPAtlas 3.0; 1,987 linked to MIBiG 4.0 biosynthetic gene clusters) with a manually curated, strain-anchored Tier B gold standard (N = 141 verified commercial bacterial products retained from 213 audited candidates across 6 industrial sectors).

Every Tier B record decouples original environmental isolation geography (isolation_country_iso3, isolation_locality, envo_biome_id) from discovering R&D institution geography (discovering_org_country), and is anchored to persistent community accessions (NPAtlas, MIBiG, UniProtKB, ChEMBL, NCBI Taxonomy, BacDive, and culture collection identifiers) alongside ECO:0000269 verbatim evidence quotes from primary discovery publications.
